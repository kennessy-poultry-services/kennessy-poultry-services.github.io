Meet the Farmer

Kenneth Chiakwa

Kenneth is an Agricultural Science and Economics teacher with over 15 years in the classroom, and a practising commercial poultry farmer running both broiler and layer operations. He holds a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Agricultural Economics and is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in the same field.

That combination — teaching the theory and running the flocks himself — shapes how Kennessy Poultry Services operates. Every bird, bag of feed, and piece of advice comes from someone who applies the science on his own farm before recommending it to anyone else.

Kenneth also authors practical farming and agricultural education guides under Kennessy eBooks, distributed to farmers and students across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Journey

From classroom to commercial farm gate

Early Career

Teaching Agricultural Science & Economics

Kenneth began teaching Agricultural Science and Economics in Enugu State, building a foundation in crop and livestock principles that would later shape the farm.

Graduate Studies

B.Sc. & M.Sc. in Agricultural Economics (ESUT)

Formal study in agricultural economics deepened the farm's approach to costing, planning and sustainable production — now pursuing a Ph.D. in the same field.

Farm Established

Broiler & Layer Operations Begin

Commercial poultry farming started with broiler and layer flocks, applying classroom knowledge directly to daily farm management.

Publishing

Kennessy eBooks Launched

Practical farming guides — covering broiler and layer production, natural poultry health, and fish farming — published for farmers and students nationwide.

Today

Kennessy Poultry Services

The farm now supplies live birds, feed and manure directly to the public, alongside consultancy for new and growing poultry farmers.

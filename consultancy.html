Frequently Asked

Consultancy questions, answered

Do I need to already own birds to book a consultation?+

No. Many clients book the Starter Consult before buying their first birds, so we can plan housing and budget together first.

Can consultations happen by phone or video call?+

Yes. In-person farm visits are available within Enugu State, and phone or video consultations can be arranged for farmers further away.

Do you work with schools and agricultural clubs?+

Yes — Group Training sessions can be tailored for agricultural science classes and can align with WAEC/NECO-style content on request.

What if my problem isn't listed in a package?+

Message us on WhatsApp with details — most farm issues fit under one of the packages above, and we'll point you to the right one.
